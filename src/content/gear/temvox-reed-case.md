A dedicated reed case gives your practice reeds a regular place in your instrument bag. The Temvox case is an option for students who want to keep their reeds together and make it easier to rotate between them.

Reeds are not included. Check the selected case’s dimensions against the reeds you use, especially for larger instruments. Follow the case’s care instructions when storing reeds after playing.
