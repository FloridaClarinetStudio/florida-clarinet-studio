D'Addario Reserve mouthpiece patches are a useful accessory for clarinet and saxophone students who want a more comfortable contact point for their upper teeth. A patch helps protect the mouthpiece from tooth wear and gives the teeth a secure place to rest during practice.

This listing is for the **black, five-pack**. Follow the package instructions when applying a patch, and replace it when it becomes worn or starts to lift. If the added thickness changes how your setup feels, bring it to a lesson so your teacher can help check the fit.
