This Rovner ligature is an option for tenor saxophone students replacing a worn ligature or refining their mouthpiece setup. A secure, comfortable fit makes it easier to position the reed consistently for practice and rehearsal.

Look for **V-2R**. This size commonly fits hard rubber tenor saxophone mouthpieces. Mouthpiece dimensions vary, so check your exact mouthpiece against [Rovner’s fit guide](https://rovnerproducts.com/most-common-fit-guide/) before ordering. Bring your current setup to a lesson if you would like help choosing.
