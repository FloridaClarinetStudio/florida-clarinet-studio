## About Robin's Teaching

Robin Ryon serves Melbourne, Palm Bay, Viera, and the Space Coast of Central Florida with lessons tailored to each student's goals and needs. He helps students prepare for All-County, All-State, solo and ensemble, auditions, chair placement, and stronger everyday musicianship.

## Programs

### Clarinet Lessons

Designed to take students from good to great, from beginners through advanced and professional musicians.

### Saxophone Lessons

Classical and jazz saxophone instruction with the same focused, individualized teaching approach.

### Band Director Mentoring

Woodwind sectionals, full-band clinics, audition preparation, leadership training, and practical support for school music programs.

### Music Theory and Composition

Musicianship support for students who want to understand how music works, not just play the notes.

## Pricing

- **At Robin's Palm Bay teaching location:** $35 per 30 minutes or $70 per hour.
- **At your house:** $40 per 30 minutes or $80 per hour, based on schedule and availability.
- **School clinics:** available for clarinet, saxophone, woodwind sectionals, full band clinics, and audition preparation.
- **Online lessons:** Zoom, Discord, or WhatsApp options are available at the Palm Bay lesson rate.
